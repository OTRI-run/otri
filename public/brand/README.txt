OTRI brand kit · Open Trail Running Index · https://otri.run

You may use these files to refer to OTRI: in an article, on a race's website next to results
scored with OTRI, in a talk, in an app that uses the OTRI API. You need not ask.

Please
- use the files as they are: do not redraw, recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects;
- keep clear space around the logo of at least half the height of the mark;
- do not show it smaller than 24 px high (mark) or 120 px wide (logo with the full name);
- use the colour logo on white or very light backgrounds, the "on-dark" or white one on dark
  backgrounds and photographs, the black one where only one colour can be printed.

Please do not
- suggest that OTRI approves, certifies, sanctions or sponsors a race, a product or a runner.
  OTRI scores courses and results with an open method; it approves nothing. "Scored with OTRI"
  says what happened. "OTRI certified" or "OTRI approved" does not exist;
- use the logo as, or as part of, your own logo, app icon or product name.

Files
  otri-logo*            the mark, the wordmark and the full name
  otri-logo-compact*    the mark and the wordmark, where space is tight
  otri-mark*            the mark alone: icons, avatars, small sizes
  otri-avatar*          a square profile picture that survives being cut to a circle
  otri-badge-scored*    "Scored with OTRI", for results pages
  (no suffix) colour · -on-dark colour for dark backgrounds · -white · -black

Colours   blue #2563eb · deep blue #1d4ed8 · sky #60a5fa · cyan #06b6d4 · ink #0b1220
Letters   the wordmark is set in Inter Bold, the full name in JetBrains Mono Bold (both SIL OFL),
          drawn as outlines: the files need no font.

The OTRI code and methodology are open source (see the repository for their licences). The name
and the logo are not part of that licence: they identify the project.
