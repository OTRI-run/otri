OTRI brand kit · Open Trail Running Index · https://otri.run

You may use these files to refer to OTRI: in an article, on a race's website next to results
scored with OTRI, in a talk, in an app that uses the OTRI API. You need not ask.

The mark is a closed ring — the letter O — with a ridge inside it and a volt dot on the summit.
It is a symbol, not a letter: the wordmark always spells OTRI in full, so the name can never be
read as "TRI". Use the mark alone only where OTRI is already named.

Please
- use the files as they are: do not redraw, recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects;
- keep clear space around the logo of at least half the height of the mark;
- do not show the mark below 20 px, or the logo with the full name below 160 px wide;
- use the colour logo on light backgrounds, the on-dark one on dark grounds and photographs,
  the white one on colour, the black one where only one ink prints.

Please do not
- suggest that OTRI approves, certifies, sanctions or sponsors a race, a product or a runner.
  OTRI scores courses and results with an open method; it approves nothing. "Scored with OTRI"
  says what happened. "OTRI certified" or "OTRI approved" does not exist;
- use the logo as, or as part of, your own logo, app icon or product name.

Files
  otri-logo*            the mark, the name and what it stands for
  otri-logo-compact*    the mark and the name, where space is tight
  otri-mark*            the mark alone: the ring, the ridge and the summit
  otri-avatar*          a square profile picture that survives being cut to a circle
  otri-badge-scored*    "Scored with OTRI", for results pages
  otri-share-card       the link preview image
  (no suffix) colour · -on-dark for dark grounds · -white · -black

Colours   ink #0b1319 · graphite #0b131a · night #05090d · paper #f1f5f7
          volt #cbf53f (the one action to press, always with ink on it) · cyan #46dcea (data, links)
Letters   the wordmark is Space Grotesk Bold, the full name IBM Plex Mono (both SIL OFL);
          the wordmark is drawn as outlines, so it needs no font.

The OTRI code and methodology are open source (see the repository for their licences). The name
and the logo are not part of that licence: they identify the project.
