# Code of Conduct

OTRI is a community project. We want technical disagreement and strong debate without personal attacks.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas, methods, data, and code rather than people.
- Assume good faith while still checking evidence.
- Welcome contributors with different levels of technical and running experience.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks, threats, or intimidation.
- Deliberate publication of private information.
- Fraudulent data submissions.
- Attempts to manipulate OTRI scores through fabricated or knowingly misleading data.

Maintainers may moderate discussions and contributions to protect the project and its participants.
