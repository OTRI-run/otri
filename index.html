01 — WHY OTRI

Same distance.
Different challenge.

A 50 km race can be fast and runnable, or steep, technical and mountainous. Finish time is only one measurement.

Distance, elevation, terrain, course design, field strength, weather, altitude and other factors can change what that time means.

02 — DATA ARCHITECTURE

More data.
Less guesswork.

OTRI is built around legitimately obtained race-result data and documented course information. Every score should be traceable to its inputs and scoring version.

04 — THE OTRI STANDARD

Not a black box.
Built to be inspected.

These principles define how OTRI is intended to operate.

01OPEN

Source code and methodology are public.

02TRANSPARENT

Scores can be inspected and explained.

03REPRODUCIBLE

The same inputs and scoring version produce the same result.

04TRACEABLE

Datasets record provenance and permissions.

05PRIVACY-CONSCIOUS

Collect and publish only what is necessary.

06INDEPENDENT

OTRI develops its own methodology and data infrastructure.

05 — FOR ORGANIZERS

Official results
are the starting point.

Provide race details and official results. OTRI is designed to validate the inputs and calculate the index — organizers do not enter a performance score.

  • Participant results
  • Finish time and ranking
  • Distance and elevation
  • GPX/course data where available
