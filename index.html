01 — WHY

Same distance.
Different challenge.

A 50 km race can be fast and runnable, or steep, technical and mountainous.

Distance, elevation, terrain, course design, field strength, weather and altitude can change what the finish time means.

02 — METHOD

Open method.
Traceable result.

OTRI is built so the scoring process can be understood, tested and reproduced. Official results and documented course data are validated before scoring.
